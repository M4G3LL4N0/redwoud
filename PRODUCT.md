# Product Context

## Product name
REDWOUD

## Product purpose
REDWOUD is an AI-native intelligence platform that transforms global events, economic indicators, market signals, geopolitical developments, and public information into structured, actionable intelligence.

## Mission
Make global intelligence accessible, structured, and actionable for anyone making decisions in a complex world.

## Vision
Become the global intelligence layer for the modern economy and the default platform for understanding what is happening in the world, why it matters, and what may happen next.

## Core problem
People face information overload from fragmented news, data, and signals. They can see events, but often cannot quickly understand:
- what matters
- what is noise
- what risks are rising
- what consequences may follow

## Solution
REDWOUD aggregates and interprets global information with AI, then presents it through a modern intelligence dashboard with explanations, live feeds, maps, briefings, filters, and risk context.

## Initial product
A free public website with:
- live intelligence feed
- global event map
- daily AI briefing
- trend summaries
- topic filters
- region filters
- “why this matters” explanations

## Expansion
Paid features for professionals and enterprises:
- custom alerts
- saved dashboards
- advanced filtering
- entity tracking
- premium briefings
- historical intelligence search
- export tools
- scenario analysis
- forecast tools
- team collaboration
- organization workspaces
- enterprise integrations
