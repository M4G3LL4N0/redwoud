# REDWOUD Full Spec

## Startup overview
REDWOUD is an AI-powered real-time global intelligence platform that turns worldwide data, news, market signals, economic indicators, geopolitical developments, and public information into structured, actionable intelligence.

## Mission
Make global intelligence accessible, structured, and actionable for anyone making decisions in a complex world.

## Vision
Become the global intelligence layer for the modern economy.

## Problem solved
Users face fragmented, overwhelming information without structured interpretation.

## Solution
REDWOUD transforms raw events and signals into clear intelligence with context, risk framing, and forward-looking insight.
